## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for protecting [Business Name]'s information assets — including customer data, financial records, employee information, and business systems — from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access company systems or data.

This policy is informed by the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)** and aligned, where practical, with common control expectations found in **ISO/IEC 27001**.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all information assets owned, leased, or managed by [Business Name], including:
- Computers, servers, and mobile devices
- Cloud services and applications (email, file storage, accounting software, etc.)
- Physical records containing sensitive information
- Networks and connectivity (Wi-Fi, VPN, remote access)

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Owner / Management | Approves policy, allocates resources, sets risk tolerance |
| IT/Security Lead (or outsourced provider) | Implements technical controls, monitors systems, responds to incidents |
| All Employees | Follow policy, report suspicious activity, complete required training |

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Access Control
- Access to systems and data is granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis.
- Unique user accounts are required for all staff — no shared logins.
- Access is reviewed at least annually and revoked immediately upon termination or role change.

### 4.2 Data Protection
- Sensitive data (customer PII, payment information, financial records) must be encrypted in transit and at rest where technically feasible.
- Data is classified into at minimum two tiers: **Public** and **Confidential**, with handling rules for each.
- Confidential data is not stored on personal devices or unapproved cloud services.

### 4.3 Device and Endpoint Security
- All company devices must use up-to-date antivirus/anti-malware protection.
- Operating systems and software must be patched within [30/60/90] days of release, prioritized by severity.
- Devices must be locked or logged off when unattended.

### 4.4 Password and Authentication
- Passwords must meet minimum complexity requirements (length, character variety) or be replaced by passphrase-based policies.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, financial systems, and any system storing confidential data.

### 4.5 Network Security
- Business Wi-Fi networks must use WPA2/WPA3 encryption at minimum.
- Guest networks are segregated from business networks.
- Remote access requires a VPN or equivalent secure connection.

### 4.6 Incident Response
- All suspected security incidents (lost devices, phishing clicks, unauthorized access) must be reported to [designated contact] within 24 hours.
- Document your incident response steps in a separate Incident Response Plan (see the README for planned additions).

### 4.7 Security Awareness
- All employees complete security awareness training upon onboarding and annually thereafter.
- Phishing simulation exercises are conducted periodically to reinforce training.

### 4.8 Third-Party and Vendor Management
- Vendors with access to confidential data must be assessed for security practices before onboarding.
- Data processing agreements are required where applicable (e.g., under privacy regulations like PIPEDA/GDPR).

## 5. Compliance and Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination, and may carry legal or regulatory consequences depending on the nature of the violation.

## 6. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually, or following a significant security incident or change in business operations.
